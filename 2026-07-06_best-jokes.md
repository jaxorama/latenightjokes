# Best Late-Night Jokes — Week of July 6–12, 2026

**Note:** This was an unusually thin week for material — Fallon, Meyers, Bill Maher, John Oliver, and SNL are all on summer break, and Colbert's *Late Show* has ended entirely. Most of the week's jokes came from Jimmy Kimmel's guest-hosted slot and The Daily Show's rotating correspondent hosts (Stewart only anchors Mondays now).

## Jimmy Kimmel Live! (guest-hosted by Tiffany Haddish, all week)

1. On Trump's sparsely attended state fair appearance:
   > "Trump said the event was going to be, and I quote, 'packed to the brim.' Well, let's see how that worked out for him." *(cut to near-empty venue footage)*

2. On Trump delaying his July 4th speech for weather:
   > "Trump was forced to delay his Fourth of July speech due to the hot and stormy weather. In fact, some MAGA fans had to seek shelter in the African-American history museum. You're welcome, again."

3. On Trump personally calling FIFA president Gianni Infantino about a player suspension before a World Cup match:
   > Called it "the first time he's ever defended a Black person" — and noted it was surprising since Trump "doesn't know anything about soccer."

4. On Team USA losing the match Trump tried to intervene in:
   > "It seems like everything Trump touches dies."

5. Meta-joke after the White House reportedly responded to her "bruised hands" joke from the night before:
   > "The president is very focused on issues that matter right now, and apparently, I'm one of them."
